<h3 align="center">PathoVision</h3>

###

<p align="left">(Along with Kausikan Dinakaran)</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Introduction:</h4>

###

<p align="left">The goal of this project is to develop a deep learning model that can accurately classify chest X-ray images into three classes: pneumonia, COVID-19, and normal (healthy lungs). The model should be able to analyze the X-ray images and identify the presence or absence of pneumonia or COVID-19 infection, as well as differentiate between the two conditions. Then ask survey (Symptoms) to the patient and book an appointment to the doctor.</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Tools and models:</h4>

###

<p align="left">Languages: Python(Django), JavaScript, HTML, CSS.<br>Platform: pychram, Visual studio, Jupyter Notbook Github,SQLlite.<br>Modals:<br>ResNet50<br>ResNet101<br>Inception Modal<br>Efficient Net</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Conclusion:</h4>

###

<p align="left">Patients can easily determine if they have COVID-19 or Pneumonia by using this website. This is crucial because it allows them to receive the assistance they require more quickly. It goes beyond simply receiving a diagnosis, though. Patients can also make an online appointment with a doctor and respond to a few questions on their symptoms on the website. This aids medical professionals in comprehending the situation even before they see the patient. In this way, patients can receive the proper care more quickly when they do see the doctor. This website aims to make healthcare more user-friendly and efficient for all parties involved, not just those who are interested in technology. Patients benefit from quicker assistance, and physicians may concentrate on what really matters taking care of patients.</p>

<p align="left">1. Tableau Desktop<br><br>Tableau Desktop served as the main tool for creating and designing the interactive dashboards Medal Tally and Game Analysis (Athletics). <h3 align="center">Medical Assistant Chatbot</h3>

###

<p align="center">Research (DOI: 10.32628/CSEIT195271)</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Introduction:</h4>

###

<p align="left">The healthcare sector is undergoing a significant transformation driven by the twin forces of the Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI). <br> These technologies open up new possibilities for patient monitoring, diagnostics and treatment workflows. The proposed system integrates IoT-based sensors (measuring vital signs such as body temperature and heart rate) with an AI-driven chat-bot to provide interactive medical assistance. <br> In this application, patients may speak input (e.g., symptoms), which the system converts to text, processes via natural language processing (NLP) and text-mining techniques to identify medical terms and correlate them with sensor data to recommend remedies or flag concerns.<br> The motivation is to assist both patients and healthcare professionals by enabling early detection of conditions (e.g., various fevers) and enhancing patient engagement and monitoring.</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Tools and Techniques Used:</h4>

###

<p align="left">The key components and tools include:<br><br>Hardware / IoT sensors:<br>A Raspberry Pi micro-computer acts as the central processing node for capturing and communicating sensor data. <br>A body-temperature sensor (LM35) used for precise measurement of a patient’s body temperature. <br>A pulse/heartbeat sensor module for tracking heart-rate / pulse rate from finger or ear placement.<br><br>Natural Language Processing & Chat-bot logic:<br>Voice input is first converted to text. Then preprocessing, tokenization, noun-phrase extraction, identification of medical terms, normalization to medical concepts are applied. <br>The chat-bot uses a knowledge base of symptom-to-disease associations (for fevers like chicken-pox, malaria, viral fever, septicemia etc) to answer user queries based on detected symptoms + sensor readings. <br><br>System integration:<br>The IoT sensor data (temperature, pulse) is sent to the cloud platform, where it is analyzed and combined with the NLP output to determine health status. <br>The overall system modules include (i) module for sensor data capture, (ii) module for voice/text-processing and term extraction, (iii) chat-bot module for question-answering / remedy suggestion.</p>

<h4 align="left">Conclusion:</h4>

###

<p align="left">The system demonstrates how IoT and AI (specifically chat-bots and NLP) can work together to deliver proactive healthcare support. The integration of sensor data and symptom-based text analytics enables a personalized interactive assistant that can detect types of fever and monitor vital signs. The authors argue that ubiquitous communication among devices and humans is becoming feasible in healthcare environments. <br><br>Who benefits / Beneficiaries:<br>Patients: They receive easier access to preliminary health screening (via sensors + chat-bot) and can be more engaged in their health monitoring, potentially catching problems earlier.<br>Healthcare providers / Clinics: The system can offload routine queries, monitor patients remotely, and provide alerts based on sensor + symptom data, improving workflow and patient care.<br>Researchers / Developers in Healthcare Technology: The approach provides a template for combining IoT + NLP in healthcare chat-bots, offering avenues for further enhancement (e.g., expanding disease coverage, improving accuracy).<br>Public Health / Remote Care Settings: In remote or underserved areas where access to doctors is limited, such systems help bridge the gap by providing low-cost, automated assistance and monitoring.<br><br>Overall, this research points toward a more connected, intelligent healthcare support ecosystem, improving responsiveness, scalability and patient empowerment.</p>
